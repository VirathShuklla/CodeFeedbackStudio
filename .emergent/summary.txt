*   **Moderation Workflow**:
    *   Moderators review all failed submissions and a 10% random sample of others.

    *   Module Leaders review and resolve moderation issues.
*   **Student Gamification**:
    *   An XP and badge system for students based on fixing issues found in their code.
    *   Badges page to view achievements.
*   **Feedback Templates**: Allow Markers to save and reuse common feedback comments.

**Phase 3: Analytics & Reporting (Future)**
*   **Analytics**: Course-specific dashboards for Students (progress) and Markers/Leaders (class-wide stats).
